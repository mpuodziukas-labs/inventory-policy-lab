# Recommendation

Keep the 95% cycle service level target and the policy formulas for most SKUs: 31 of 40 SKUs meet target within 3 points in simulation, with a mean achieved cycle service level of 94.0% and a mean fill rate of 99.2%.

## Cost of a higher target

Moving all 40 SKUs from 95.0% to 97.5% raises safety stock from 32,734 to 39,005 units and total annual holding cost from GBP 27,316 to GBP 29,594 (holding cost is an assumed rate on median selling price).

## Forecasting

Simple exponential smoothing has the lowest overall WAPE (49.7%) against 51.7% for the moving average and 63.4% for the naive forecast, and beats naive on 40 of 40 SKUs. Use it for the weekly demand outlook.

All numbers are regenerated by `make regen` from `config.json` and the vendored data.
